The NYSC Social, Opportunity & Community Platform

Before NYSC → Camp → PPA → CDS → Career → POP

1. PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
1.1 Product Overview

Kopa Connect is a mobile-first social and opportunity platform designed specifically around the NYSC journey in Nigeria.

The platform helps prospective and serving Corps Members:

Navigate camp
Connect with people around them
Share and discover updates
Find jobs and income opportunities
Showcase skills
Discover vendors and services
Chat with other Corps Members
Participate in communities
Discover events
Maintain a professional/social profile

Kopa Connect should feel like a mobile application, not a conventional website.

2. Core Product Philosophy

The product is built around five core pillars.

1. 🏕 Camp Experience

Everything a PCM needs during orientation camp.

2. 👥 Connect to People Around You

Discover Corps Members around your:

Camp
State
LGA

3. 📰 Open Updates

4. 💼 Jobs & Skills

Freelance work
Remote opportunities
Internships
Local income opportunities

Users can also showcase their skills.

5. 🛍 Marketplace

16. PPA Location
